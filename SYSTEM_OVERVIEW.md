# Servease Overview

Servease is an online platform that helps customers find and book trusted local service providers. Customers can create an account, browse services, request bookings, track booking status, leave reviews, and verify their email with OTP for security. Service providers can apply, submit required documents, manage their profile, handle booking requests, and view customer feedback. Admins review provider applications, manage users, services, reports, settings, and platform activity. The goal is to make local services easier, safer, and more organized for everyone.
